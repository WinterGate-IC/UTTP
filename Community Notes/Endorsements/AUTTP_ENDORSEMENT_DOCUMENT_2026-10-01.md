# ENDORSEMENT OF THE ANTI-UTTP COALITION (AUTTP)

**Issued By:** WinterGate Intelligence Collective
**Date:** 2026-10-01
**Classification:** Public — Endorsement

---

## ENDORSEMENT STATEMENT

The WinterGate Intelligence Collective formally endorses the **Anti-UTTP Coalition (AUTTP)** and recognizes it as a legitimate, structured, and increasingly professional opposition movement to the UTTP network.

We endorse AUTTP not because we share every tactic or every member, but because we have observed a clear and documented shift in their operational posture. They are moving away from the chaotic, reactive vigilantism that has historically plagued anti-troll efforts and toward a disciplined, evidence-based, and platform-focused advocacy model. This is the same shift we made within our own operations. We recognize it when we see it.

**They are doing the work that platforms have failed to do. And they are doing it better than most.**

---

## WHY WE ENDORSE AUTTP

### 1. They Have Shifted to Structured, Evidence-Based Objectives

AUTTP is no longer just "fighting trolls." They have published a clear mission with concrete, actionable goals:

- **BAN UTTP** — Demand that YouTube, TikTok, and all major platforms permanently ban UTTP-affiliated accounts and implement coordinated harassment detection .
- **PROTECT CREATORS** — Fight for faster appeal systems, human review for coordinated harassment cases, and stronger protections for small creators .
- **RAISE AWARENESS** — Expose UTTP's tactics to the wider creator community so every creator knows how to document harassment, appeal false strikes, and report coordinated abuse .

This is not vague outrage. This is a policy platform. It is the same kind of structured objective-setting that we applied to our own archive project.

### 2. They Are Documenting and Directing Others to Document

AUTTP explicitly tells its supporters:

> *"If you're targeted by UTTP, screenshot everything — comments, report notifications, timestamps. Documented patterns are much harder for platforms to ignore than individual appeals."* 

This is the **documentation gap** that we identified as the core problem. AUTTP has recognized it independently and is closing it through community action. They are teaching creators to build the same kind of evidence trail that we build in our archives.

### 3. They Are Organized and Formalized

The AUTTP server rules demonstrate a level of discipline that most anti-troll groups never achieve:

- **No Trolls or UTTP** — immediate ban for affiliation .
- **Protect and Attack** — raiding is forbidden *unless* it targets a UTTP server .
- **No Malicious Links** — no phishing, IP grabbers, or scams .
- **No CP or CSAM** — strict ban, no exceptions .
- **No Misinformation** — no fake raid alerts or rumors .
- **No Betrayal** — no alliances with UTTP members, former or current .

This is a **rules of engagement framework**. It is the same kind of operational discipline that we apply to our own campaigns. They are not a mob. They are a coalition with standards.

### 4. They Have Built Public Infrastructure

AUTTP operates a public-facing website at `keane3029-lab.github.io/auttp-site/` that includes:

- **A Threat Briefing** documenting UTTP's tactics, including mass false reporting, harassment raids, community infiltration, and platform loophole abuse .
- **A Mission Page** with the three core objectives and a manifesto .
- **A Petition Page** with a live Change.org petition demanding platform action .
- **A "How to Help" section** directing supporters to document attacks, report to platforms, and share the petition .

They have built the infrastructure of a serious advocacy organization. They are not just complaining on Discord. They are building a public record and a public campaign.

### 5. They Are Exposing UTTP's Tactics Publicly

AUTTP's threat briefing is a **miniature version of our own archive project**. It documents:

- **Mass False Reporting** — coordinated reports to trigger automated strikes .
- **Harassment Raids** — organized toxic comments, hate speech, and impersonation .
- **Community Infiltration** — accounts that blend in to gather intel and seed distrust .
- **Platform Loophole Abuse** — exploiting the gap between automated enforcement and human review .

This is exactly the kind of documentation that platforms and law enforcement need. AUTTP is producing it independently.

### 6. They Are Reaching the Platforms

AUTTP's Change.org petition is live and gaining momentum . It demands:

- Stronger AI-driven spam detection
- Stricter account verification
- Effective reporting mechanisms
- Platforms to prioritize this issue and adapt to evolving threats like UTTP's coordinated harassment campaigns 

They are not just talking to each other. They are talking to the platforms. And they are building a public pressure campaign to force action.

## WHAT WE ARE ENDORSING

We endorse AUTTP's **direction, structure, and methodology**.

- **Their direction**: From chaotic retaliation to structured advocacy.
- **Their structure**: From scattered servers to a formalized coalition with rules and objectives.
- **Their methodology**: From screaming into the void to documenting evidence, building public records, and pressuring platforms.

We are **not** endorsing every individual member or every action taken by every affiliated group. We are endorsing the **movement's trajectory** and its **strategic shift toward professionalism**.

---

## WHY THIS MATTERS

The documentation gap that allowed UTTP to operate with impunity for years was never just about our archive. It was about the **absence of coordinated, evidence-based opposition**. AUTTP is filling that gap.

They are doing what platforms won't do, what law enforcement can't do at scale, and what scattered victims couldn't do alone. They are **building the case**.

We endorse them because we recognize the same principle we operate by: **the record is the weapon.** AUTTP has figured that out. They are building their own record. And they are doing it with discipline.
