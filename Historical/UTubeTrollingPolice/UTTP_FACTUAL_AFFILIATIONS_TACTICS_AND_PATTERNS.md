## SECTION 1: UTTP'S ORIGIN AND FOUNDING

### 1.1 Founder: Thomas Parkinson (Tommy Parky)

UTTP was founded on **February 12, 2011**, by Thomas Parkinson, also known as "Tommy Parky" or "Tranny Pedo" . The group was created on Google+ under the guise of "protecting YouTube users from harassment" .

Multiple sources document that Parkinson created UTTP for the purpose of **grooming 11-year-old boys** . Parkinson is also documented as having been arrested in 2015 on charges of drunk and disorderly conduct, threatening to kill two Gardaí, resisting arrest, indecent behavior, and failing to appear in court . He is widely reported to be in jail as of 2025-2026 .

### 1.2 Founding Purpose

The group's documented founding purpose was not "anti-trolling" but **child grooming and gore spam** . Sources state Parkinson "originally made the UTTP to groom 11 year old boys and livestream his trans bottom surgery, spam random sites with gore and flex how many children he groomed" .

In **December 2025**, Canada formally listed the **764 Network** as a terrorist group . The designation places 764 within the realm of terrorism rather than treating it solely as a criminal or child-exploitation phenomenon .
