- Drunk and disorderly conduct
- Threatening to kill two Gardaí

- Failure to appear in court

He is widely reported as **in jail** as of 2025-2026 .

### 1.4 Strategic Significance

UTTP was not founded as a troll group that was later infiltrated by extremists. It was founded by a documented child groomer, for the purpose of grooming. This origin is central to understanding everything that follows.
