## 1. EXECUTIVE SUMMARY

UTTP (UTubeTrollPolice) is a decentralized anti-fandom trolling group founded on **February 12–13, 2011**, by **Thomas Parkinson** (Tommy Parky / southparkstudiosable). The group is currently **active but fragmented**, operating as a brand or "trolling style" rather than a unified organization.

**Current operational status:**

| Metric | Status |
|---|---|
| **Primary platforms** | YouTube, Discord, X (Twitter), Telegram |
| **Secondary platforms** | Tumblr, Reddit, TikTok, Telegram, Bluesky |
| **Leadership model** | Decentralized — multiple self-appointed "officers" and splinter leaders |
| **Current known factions** | UTTP Official, UTTP Empire, Retro-UTTP, YFGA, ZNTP, THTDC |
| **FBI investigations** | Confirmed — 250+ open investigations tied to leadership as of 2025 |
| **Central command** | None — fragmented into splinters with no unified structure |
| **Estimated core membership** | Unknown — predominantly minors under 14 |

The group is **not collapsing** — it is **dispersing**. It has adapted to platform bans by migrating across platforms and splintering into cells, making it harder to dismantle as a single entity.

## 2. CURRENT PLATFORM PRESENCE

### 2.1 YouTube

**Primary operational platform.** UTTP uses botnets to flood comment sections of popular YouTubers and trending videos.

**Known channel:**

| Channel | Status | Link |
|---|---|---|
| The UTUBETROLLPOLICE | Active | `https://www.youtube.com/@theutubetrollpolice1448` |
| UtubeTrollPoliceTeam | Historical (2011) | `https://www.youtube.com/user/UtubeTrollPoliceTeam/` |

**Current tactics:**
- Comment spamming with variations of "UTTP is better than [Target Fandom/Creator]"
- Bypassing filters using backwards text and hidden Unicode characters
- Organized raids against specific fandoms (Murder Drones, Gacha Life, Mapping, Amazing Digital Circus)
- Doxxing and sharing illegal material links

**Target list (current):**

### 10.2 The "Brand vs. Organization" Reality

> *"By 2026, the UTTP exists more as a brand or a 'trolling style' than a unified organization."*

UTTP is no longer a centralized group. It is a **brand name** that splinter cells adopt. This makes it:

- **Harder to dismantle** — no single point of failure
- **Harder to track** — new cells appear constantly
- **Harder to hold accountable** — leadership rotates and fragments

### 10.3 The Minor Problem

> *"It is a group of channels on YouTube that first appeared in 2011 by Tommy Parky, and is mainly composed of children under the age of 14."*

UTTP's membership is predominantly **children**. This creates a legal and ethical complexity: the group is criminal in its activities, but many of its members are minors who are themselves victims of grooming and exploitation by older members.
