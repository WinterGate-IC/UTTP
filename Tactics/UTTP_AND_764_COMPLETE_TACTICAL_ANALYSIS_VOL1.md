## SECTION 1: ORIGINS AND ORGANIZATIONAL EVOLUTION

### 1.1 Founding (2011)

UTTP (UTube Troll Police) was founded on **February 12, 2011**, by **Thomas Parkinson** (Tommy Parky) on Google+ . The group was framed as a vigilante initiative to "police" YouTube comment sections and report trolls .

The founding was ironic from the start. The group's methodology—mass reporting and leaving "citations" in comments—mirrored the very harassment they claimed to combat . Members prioritized "detaining" (harassing) users over legitimate moderation .

### 1.2 The Hyper Bat Coup (Early 2023)

The transition from decentralized troll group to centralized botnet organization was catalyzed by **Hyper Bat Mapping** (also known as "Emperor of Anime Sucks" or HBM) . After being rejected by veteran leader Sherbase12 in 2020, HBM used alternate accounts to infiltrate the group and oust figures like OfficerSlappy and OfficerCarnevil .

This coup replaced manual trolling with automated botnet infrastructure and targeted raids . The group rebranded as "UTTP Empire," adopted the title "Emperor of Anime Sucks," and migrated operations to Discord .

### 1.3 Internal Fracturing (2024-2026)

By 2025-2026, UTTP had fractured into **dozens of splinter groups** . The "Old Guard" and "New Wave" engaged in internal conflicts, including dox wars . Veteran members disputed whether the group was ever truly centralized, with some claiming HBM only controlled a vocal faction .

Despite fracturing, the group remained active as a **decentralized harassment network** operating through constant account creation and raids .

## SECTION 2: UTTP TACTICS — PRIMARY METHODS

### 2.1 Mass False Reporting

**Primary Tactic:** Coordinated mass false reporting .

**Method:**
- Groups of UTTP members file identical copyright or Community Guidelines reports simultaneously on a target channel .
- YouTube's automated systems interpret volume as legitimacy .
- Result: unearned strikes, removals, or terminations even when reports are fabricated .

**Strategic Goal:** Silence creators by abusing automated platform systems .

**Exploitation:** The gap between automated enforcement (hours) and human review (days/weeks) means creators lose income and visibility even when they win appeals .

### 2.2 Harassment Raids

**Secondary Tactic:** Organized raids on target channels .

**Method:**
- Coordinated toxic comments
- Hate speech
- Impersonation attempts
- Mass dislike campaigns 

**Strategic Goal:** Intimidate creators into deleting content, going private, or quitting—without needing the formal report system .

### 2.3 Community Infiltration

**Infiltration Tactic:** UTTP members create accounts that blend into creative communities .

**Method:**
- Gather intel on target creators
- Manufacture drama
- Spread false narratives before reporting campaigns begin
- Seed distrust and confusion in the community 

**Strategic Goal:** By the time raids start, distrust and confusion are already seeded .

### 2.4 Platform Loophole Abuse

**System Exploit:** UTTP specifically exploits the gap between automated enforcement and human review .

**Method:**
- Automated strikes remove content or restrict channels within hours
- Appeals process takes days or weeks
- Creators lose income and visibility even when they ultimately win

## SECTION 3: UTTP TACTICS — TECHNICAL METHODS

### 3.1 The DroneFall Strategy (2024-2026)

**Technical Escalation:** UTTP moved from manual comment posting to **DroneFall**—massive botnets to overwhelm creators .

**Method:**
- Bots programmed to target specific keywords and high-traffic videos (Glitch Productions, DannoDraws) 
- Thousands of disposable accounts bypass rate-limiting and shadow-banning 
- "Glitched" text or hidden characters avoid automated spam filters 
- Delivers signature "UTTP is better" messaging 

**Strategic Goal:** Prioritize volume and persistence over individual interaction .

**Impact:** Forced creators to rely on third-party moderation tools like ThioJoe's Spambot Crusher .

### 3.2 Botnet Operations

**Method:**
- Bots reply to every comment on a YouTube video 
- Disruptive messages: "didnt ask my content is better," "child porn is unironically better than this video," "support map pride" 
- Non-members pinged at least **189 times a day** in announcement channels 

**Strategic Goal:** Overwhelm creators and communities with automated spam .

### 3.3 Psyop Campaigns

**Method:**
- UTTP uses bots to **represent both their support and their opposition** 
- Creates false illusion of influence and fame 
- Most "seethe" about UTTP online is written by UTTP members themselves 

**Strategic Goal:** Manufacture the appearance of a larger, more influential group than actually exists .
