## 1.1 ORIGIN AND FOUNDING

UTTP (UTube Troll Police) emerged on **February 12, 2011**, founded by **Thomas Parkinson** (Tommy Parky) on Google+ . The group's stated purpose was "protecting YouTube users from harassment," but the documented reality was different. Primary sources indicate Parkinson created UTTP to **groom 11-year-old boys**, spam gore, and "flex how many children he groomed" .

Parkinson was arrested in **2015** on charges including drunk and disorderly conduct, threatening to kill two Gardaí, resisting arrest, indecent behavior, and failure to appear in court . He is widely reported to be in jail as of 2025-2026.

## 1.2 CORE TACTICS AND METHODS

### 1.2.1 Coordinated Comment Raids

UTTP's primary method is **coordinated comment raids** on YouTube videos, targeting specific creators and fandoms . These raids involve:

- **Mass account creation** — using bots and alt accounts to flood comment sections
- **Disruptive spam** — repetitive messages like "didnt ask my content is better," "child porn is unironically better than this video," and "support map pride" 
- **Caps Lock spam** — aggressive, all-caps messages designed to disrupt
- **Political and racist content** — injecting political and racial slurs into comments 

### 1.2.2 Targeted Harassment Campaigns

UTTP targets specific fandoms and communities, including :

| **Target** | **Tactic** |
|---|---|
| The Autistic Dilation Circus (TADC) | Comment raids, wiki vandalism, false identity dissemination  |
| Murder Drones | Attack declarations, comment disruption  |
| Danno Cal Drawings | Doxxing threats  |
| Fundamental Paper Education | Comment terrorism  |
| Gacha Life | Personal information theft and extortion (2020)  |
| Skibidi Toilet | Comment raids  |
| MatPat (Game Theory) | Comment spam on retirement video  |

### 1.2.3 Doxxing and Swatting

UTTP has threatened to **leak home addresses** of creators who oppose them . The group is documented as engaging in **swatting** — making false reports to emergency services to trigger armed police responses at targets' homes .

### 1.2.4 Death Threats and Violent Threats

UTTP is documented as issuing **murder threats**, **death threats**, and other violent threats against individuals . This includes threatening to "grape" (rape) people on Discord and Twitter .

### 1.2.5 Bot Network Operations

UTTP operates a **botnet** that:
- Spams YouTube comment sections 
- Uses "glitched" text to bypass automated filters 
- Engages in "depraved content" 
- Represents both support and opposition to create a false illusion of influence 

The group's bot network has been described as "dronefall" — massive botnets using AI-generated comments to overwhelm creators .

### 1.2.6 Psyop and Propaganda Tactics

UTTP engages in a "guerilla-warfare psyop campaign all across the internet" . Most "seethe" found about UTTP online is **written by UTTP members themselves** to create an illusion that the group is more famous and influential than it actually is .

### 1.2.7 Raiding Tactics

UTTP raids target communities by:
- Spamming gay porn videos 
- "Pizzabombing" — flooding with unwanted content 
- Trolling users in comment sections 
- Attacking community wikis and websites 

### 1.2.8 Server Structure and Recruitment

UTTP servers operate with a tiered structure :

- **Members separated from non-members** — strict access controls
- **Unverified members pinged constantly** — "unverified members would get pinged 8347854 times a day unless they proved themselves by spamming CP/self-harming" 
- **Initiation requirements** — historically required sending child porn or giving a YouTube account for spam operations
- **Kill a cat in VC** — initiation ritual **copied from 764** 
- **Rename punishments** — admins rename members to "something pedo-related" if they dislike them 

### 1.2.9 Fandom Targeting

UTTP's main targets are "shitty fandoms" . The group sends "p" (child porn) and gore to disrupt communities. Despite their attempts, many fandoms are "more than 10 times bigger than them" and UTTP's raids are often ineffective .

### 1.2.10 The "Trolling Tactics" Page

UTTP operated a site called **NoLimitsPedia (nolimitspedia.net)** that served as an Encyclopedia Dramatica clone . The site:
- Endorsed child porn on its front page 
- Hosted animal gore on the main page 
- Posted defamatory content about a website administrator named "Froot" using fake evidence 
- Was DDoSed by "teens" and reported to Cloudflare and its registrar

## 1.3 LEADERSHIP EVOLUTION

### 1.3.1 Tommy Parky Era (2011-2013)

The founding era, characterized by anti-troll activities across YouTube, Xbox Live clans, and GoAnimate .

### 1.3.2 Stephen Larson Era

After Parky's fatigue, **Stephen Larson** (StembleyTheRaccoon on Kiwi Farms) took leadership . He shifted UTTP's focus from YouTube to Google+ trolling, raiding fandoms, and community websites. His "piss-poor handling" and "shit OPSEC" led to him being "oxed" and de-platformed .

### 1.3.3 Officer Slappy Era (2018-Present)

**Officer Slappy** revived UTTP in 2018. This iteration is still the "official" version of UTTP . His version continued raiding targeted communities while orienting efforts back to YouTube-related drama .

In mid-2023, UTTP resurged under a new server called **"UTTP Empire,"** owned by **"UTTP Emperor Anime Sucks"** (also known as "HYPER BAT MAPPING") . This sub-faction is documented as being "responsible for the mass-spam" of CSAM and gore .

## 1.5 CURRENT OPERATIONAL STATUS

### 1.5.1 Decentralization

UTTP is now described as a **"decentralized harassment network"** that persists through constant account terminations . This decentralization is a survival tactic to avoid total platform bans.

### 1.5.2 AI-Driven Bot Spam

The group has moved to **"DroneFall"** — massive botnets using AI-generated comments to overwhelm creators .

### 1.5.3 Retreat to Niche Spaces

Their activity is increasingly found in smaller, insular communities. On Discord, server tags now associate UTTP with terms like **"764," "O9A," "extortion," and "selfharm"** .

### 1.5.4 AUTTP Opposition

The **Anti-UTTP (AUTTP)** movement has formalized. Groups like **"The UTTP Destroyers"** and the **"Private-Online-Safety-Alliance"** are actively working to remove UTTP from the internet. They have structured rules, public campaigns, and identifiable leadership .

## 2.2 CORE TACTICS AND METHODS

### 2.2.1 Sextortion and Coercion

764's primary method is **sextortion of minors** — typically between the ages of **10 and 17**, often girls dealing with mental health challenges . The process follows a documented pattern :

1. **Initial Contact** — Members befriend minors on platforms like Roblox, Discord, X, or Reddit
2. **Off-Platform Migration** — Conversations move to encrypted platforms like Telegram or private Discord channels
3. **Grooming** — Trust-building, romantic relationships, or coercion tactics
4. **Coercion into Acts** — Victims are pressured into:
   - Self-harm
   - Sexual acts
   - Animal torture
   - Attacks on family members
   - Suicide attempts
5. **Recording** — Acts are recorded or photographed
6. **Blackmail** — Recordings are used to coerce even more abhorrent acts

### 2.2.2 "Cut-Signing" and Blood Rituals

Victims are often pushed into **cutting themselves with razors** — "cutsigning" their username, "764," or other phrases into their skin . This serves as both proof of loyalty and a form of psychological branding.

### 2.2.3 Offline Violence

764 has evolved from online sextortion to **offline kinetic acts** :

- **Arson**

- **Stabbings**

A Swedish case involved a 14-year-old "Slain764" who attacked a woman in her 50s and an 82-year-old man, filming the attacks and circulating them .

### 2.2.4 Lorebooks and Internal Currency

764 uses **"Lorebooks"** as an internal currency and prestige system . Members earn status by producing increasingly harmful content. This gamification drives escalation.

### 2.2.5 Cross-Platform Victimisation

764 members operate across **nearly all social media platforms** :

### 2.2.6 Recruitment of Victims
